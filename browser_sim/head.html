Offline attack lab

Real encryption and a real dictionary attack run in your browser (WebCrypto PBKDF2-SHA256, AES, and a Honey Encryption prototype). The attacker only gets the ciphertext and public information. Ground truth appears only in the evaluation view. HE-v1 is the first decoy model; HE-v2 is the model revised after the attack, analyse, identify, improve loop (see the dashboard for the full experiment).
